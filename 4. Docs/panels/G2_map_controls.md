## Question it answers
Which model, field and time does the map (G1) show?

## What you see
- **Model:** IFS · AIFS (AI) · Ensemble middle · Seasonal.
- **Show:** Temperature · Wind 100 m · Solar · Rain.
- **Time (NEM):** slider + Earlier / Later. Opens at the next forecast time from now; switching model goes back to "now"
  (seasonal: first month).
- A note on what the colours mean for the current choice.

## Model and data
Same as G1. Times step 3-hourly (IFS to day 6), 6-hourly (AIFS, ensemble to day 7), then 12-hourly (ensemble),
monthly (seasonal).

## How to read it
Pick the model, then drag the slider to watch a front, heat or cloud band move.

## Limits
Choice is not remembered after a reload (starts on IFS temperature).
